Privacy policy

Last updated on 9 October 2026. This policy covers this website and the games published by Axiom Media & Tech.

Who is responsible

Axiom Media & Tech is a sole proprietorship registered in Belgium at Orchideeënstraat 19 bus 201, 2610 Antwerpen, Belgium, with enterprise number 1037.702.139. It decides how your personal data is used and is the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

For any privacy question or request, write to info@axiomtech.be.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from advertising or social media companies. The fonts are served from this site.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives the IP address of each visitor and keeps technical logs for security. Axiom Media & Tech does not receive those logs. GitHub explains its own handling of this data in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you send an email

If you write to Axiom Media & Tech, your email address and the content of your message are used to answer you and, where needed, to fix the problem you reported. Messages are kept for as long as that takes and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you write again. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in answering people who get in touch.

Email to info@axiomtech.be is forwarded to a mailbox provided by Google.

The games

Axiom Media & Tech has not published any games yet. Before a game is released, this section will list that game by name and state which data it collects, why, which other companies receive it, and how to turn optional collection off.

Games are distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those stores handle downloads and payments under their own privacy policies. Axiom Media & Tech never receives your payment card details.

Children

This website is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Any game intended for children will say so in this policy and in its store listing.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask Axiom Media & Tech to:

  • tell you which personal data it holds about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • restrict or stop a particular use of your data.

Write to info@axiomtech.be to use any of these rights. You will get an answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority at dataprotectionauthority.be, or to the authority in the EU country where you live.

Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date at the top.
